happnd Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

happnd is a duration- and habit-tracking app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. In short: everything you enter stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere by the app.

No account, no network access

happnd does not require or offer any account, sign-up, or login. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission at all, so it is not technically capable of making network requests, sending analytics, or syncing data to any server. There is no server: nothing exists for your data to be sent to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in happnd — trackers, practice sessions and their durations, labels, notes, and quantity or check-in logs — is stored in a local database on your device. The app itself never transmits it or shares it with any third party, because it has no mechanism to do so.

Android backup and device transfer

happnd lets Android include this local database in the device's own backup and device-to-device transfer, so your history can survive a new phone or a reinstall. When backup is turned on in your device settings, Android (not happnd) stores the backup in your Google account, under Google's privacy terms and with Google's encryption. You can turn backup off in your device's system settings at any time; happnd works the same either way.

Exporting your data

You can export your data as a ZIP of CSV files. The file goes only where you send it: to a location you pick in the system file picker, or to an app you choose in the share sheet. happnd does not upload it anywhere.

Uninstalling the app deletes the local database on your device (a backup that Android already stored in your Google account is managed there). You can also delete individual trackers, sessions, or labels from within the app at any time.

No analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs

happnd includes no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other third-party tracking libraries of any kind. The app does not know how you use it beyond what is visible on your own screen, because it has no way to report that information to anyone.

Sending feedback

If you choose to use the in-app "Send Feedback" option, happnd opens your device's own email app with a message pre-filled (your feedback text, the category you picked, and the app's version number). You can review, edit, or cancel it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your own email app, and it goes directly to the developer's support inbox through your email provider — not through happnd or any server it controls.

Background timer notification

While a practice session is actively running, happnd may use a foreground service (with a persistent notification, as Android requires) so timing and optional sound cues keep working if you switch away from the app. This service only keeps the running timer and audio alive; it does not collect or transmit any data.

Children's privacy

happnd is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, of any age — it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Given the app's design (local-only, no network access), any future change would need to be a deliberate, disclosed part of a new app version — not a silent one.
